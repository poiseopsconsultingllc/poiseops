Story

Why PoiseOps exists

It started with a simple question: what if your workday did not have to feel like constant catch up.

Before launching PoiseOps, I spent more than a decade in grants, finance and accounting inside universities and schools of medicine. I lived in the world of sponsored projects, shifting requirements, tight deadlines and messy handoffs between departments. I have worked with federal, state, local, public and private sponsors, and I know what it is like to be the person who has to make the numbers, the narrative and the timeline all line up.

Over those years, I became the systems person by necessity. I built and fixed workflows in Excel, Power Query, SQL, Power BI and Tableau, then moved into Python, JavaScript, AI and full software development to stop doing the same manual tasks over and over.

That path led me to build HumbleSuite, an entire business platform that replaces around 200 separate tools. PoiseOps is how I use everything I learned to make other people's workdays calmer, clearer and more sustainable.

I come from a military family and I have a deep respect for people who serve, and for the people who keep public, grant funded and community work going behind the scenes. PoiseOps is veteran friendly and comfortable in government, university and nonprofit environments where rules, funding cycles and compliance actually matter.

Why work with PoiseOps

  • I have lived on your side of the screen. I understand grants, budgets, sponsors, finance and reporting from the inside, not just as abstract data.
  • I speak both money and machines. I connect your real world constraints with Python, SQL, JavaScript, React, Excel, Power Automate, Power BI and Tableau.
  • I actually build software. I created and run HumbleSuite, so I bring real development, not just light scripting.
  • The goal is poise, not more hustle. More control and breathing room, less noise.
  • You get founder attention. The same person who listens designs, writes the code and tunes the system.
  • Pricing is right sized. Projects that pay for themselves in time saved and reduced overhead.
What I believe

The people doing the real work deserve tools and an ally on their side, not another bill and another bot. I build to hand you back your time and your control, and to put enterprise grade power in the hands of the people the software giants overlook.
