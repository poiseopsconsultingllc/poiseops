What this is

AI that fits the way you already work

This is hands on help getting AI into your day to day work without the overwhelm. I meet you where you are, with the tools you already pay for.

Most small teams are told that AI is either a magic button or a giant platform rollout. The truth sits in the middle. The real wins come from small, well placed automations and a few good habits. I help you find the spots where AI saves real time, like sorting and triaging a busy inbox, drafting a first version of a proposal, cleaning up a rough report, or pulling the key points out of a long document. Then I set those up so they run quietly in the background.

I lean on low code tools like Power Automate and connectors like Zapier where they actually help, and I am happy to use the AI features already built into Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and the apps you use every day. You do not need to learn to code, and you do not need a new pile of subscriptions. In many cases you already own the pieces and just need someone to wire them together with a steady hand.

Just as important, I help you decide where AI belongs and where it does not. Some parts of your work need a human voice and a human judgment call, and I will tell you when that is the case. The aim is poise, calm and clear control over your tools, so AI works for you instead of the other way around.

Questions

A few things people ask first

Will AI make my emails and documents sound like a robot?

No. The whole point is that your work still sounds like you. I set up AI to triage, draft, and clean up around your own voice and your real templates, then I show you how to keep a quick human check in place. You stay the author. AI just clears the busywork.

Do I need to buy expensive software to start using AI?

Usually not. Most small teams already pay for tools that can do far more than they use. I start with what you have, like Microsoft 365 and Power Automate or a Zapier plan, and only suggest spending money when a paid step clearly earns its keep. Practical AI for business should lower cost and effort, not add another bill.

What if I am not sure where AI actually belongs in my work?

That is a good place to start, and it is most of the value. A big part of AI adoption consulting is deciding where AI helps and where it does not, so you stay in control. We map your real workflows first, then put AI only where it saves real time and leave the rest alone.
