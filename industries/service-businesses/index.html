What this is

Less time at the keyboard, more time doing the work

In a service business, the owner is usually the front desk too. You take the booking, send the reminder, answer the message, chase the invoice and ask for the review, all between actual jobs. None of it is hard on its own. Together it eats your day and your evenings. I take that weight off you with simple systems that run in the background.

I organize and automate the email and messages around reservations, orders and client requests, so the right ones surface at the right time instead of buried in a noisy inbox. I set up flows for intake, reminders and feedback requests using tools like Power Automate and low code connectors, so a new booking quietly turns into a confirmation, a reminder the day before and a review request after. You stop copying details between apps by hand.

When you need a real front door, I can stand up a website, online storefront and invoicing fast, often through HumbleSuite, the all in one business platform I built. You get a clean place for customers to book and pay, and a clear view of weekly revenue, visits and key numbers without digging through spreadsheets. Everything is yours and nothing is bloated.

Common questions

A few things owners ask first

Do I have to switch all of my tools to work with you?

No. I start with the tools you already pay for, like your booking app, your inbox and your invoicing. I connect and automate around them with Power Automate and low code connectors. If something is truly missing, I can stand up a website, storefront and invoicing fast, often through HumbleSuite, but only when it actually helps.

I am a small shop with no tech person. Is this overkill?

It is the opposite of overkill. Small service businesses feel admin the hardest because the owner is also the front desk. I build simple, calm flows for bookings, reminders and follow ups so you spend less time on the keyboard and more time doing the work. You own everything and there is nothing giant to learn.

Do you only work with businesses near Colorado Springs?

I am based near Colorado Springs and meet Front Range businesses in person, but I serve service businesses across the whole country remotely. Veteran owned businesses and public sector or grant funded teams are especially welcome.
