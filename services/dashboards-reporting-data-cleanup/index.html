What this is

Plain reporting work, done right. I take the data you already have and make it show you something useful.

Most teams are not short on data. They are short on data they trust. The same number lives in three spreadsheets and reads differently in each one. Someone spends a Friday afternoon copying cells into a slide deck, and by Monday it is already stale. I fix that. I clean and structure your data with Power Query and SQL, then build a dashboard on top of it in Power BI or Tableau so the numbers stay current on their own.

The result is a single screen that answers the questions you ask most. Revenue this month against last month. Grant draws against budget. Operations and performance at a glance, with the slow parts colored so you see them without hunting. When the underlying data refreshes, the dashboard refreshes. No copy and paste, no Friday afternoon spent rebuilding the same report you built the month before.

I also connect the pieces that should already talk to each other. SQL data and spreadsheets, your accounting export and your operations tracker, so the right numbers flow where they belong instead of being keyed in twice. And when you need to plan ahead, I build forecasts and what if views for slow months, delayed payments or funding pauses, so a hard stretch is something you saw coming. If you want this kind of reporting wired into the everyday running of the business, I also built HumbleSuite at humblesuite.com, an all in one platform that carries a lot of this for you.

How it works

A calm, four step path from messy data to a dashboard you trust.

1
Discovery
We talk through the questions you ask most and where your data lives now. I look at the spreadsheets, the SQL, the exports and the messy bits, so I know exactly what I am working with before I touch anything.
2
Design
I sketch the dashboard and the layout with you. We agree on which numbers matter, how they should read, and whether Power BI or Tableau fits your team best. No surprises later.
3
Build
I clean and structure the data with Power Query and SQL, connect the sources that should talk, and build the dashboard on top. You see it taking shape and tell me what to adjust as it grows.
4
Poise and tuning
We tune it once it is live. I add forecasts and what if views, tidy the labels, and walk you through refreshing it yourself. You leave with a setup that holds steady and a guide so it stays that way.
